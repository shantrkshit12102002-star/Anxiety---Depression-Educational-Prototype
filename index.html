Amavata Severity-Based Research Assistant

A structured educational interface for recording selected symptoms, calculating a project-defined symptom-burden score, and preparing a clinician-review report.

Browser-based · No account required · Local-only processing
Important clinical limitationThis is not a diagnostic, prescribing, or treatment-selection device. Severity thresholds are illustrative and have not been clinically validated. Do not start, stop, or change treatment based on this report. Do not enter names, phone numbers, addresses, or other identifying information.
